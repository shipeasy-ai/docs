Plan levers are platform knobs we control centrally — they live in the plan config, not per project. Changing a plan value re-paces every project on that plan through the next KV rebuild, with no per-project migration.

<Callout type="warn" title="Numbers here are illustrative">
  The tables below reflect current defaults but can move. **Your dashboard shows your live limits
  and usage** — check **Project → Billing** for the authoritative numbers and where you stand
  against them.
</Callout>

## Read path

The biggest functional difference between tiers is how often the SDK refreshes its rules.

| Knob               | Free                          | Pro                           | Business                      | Enterprise                    |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Poll interval      | ~300s                         | ~60s                          | ~30s                          | ~30s                          |
| Analysis frequency | Daily                         | Daily                         | Daily                         | Daily                         |
| Propagation model  | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge |

The poll interval is the worst-case lag between an edit going live at the edge and a given SDK instance noticing it on its next background poll. Evaluation itself is always local and instant — see [Evaluation & caching](/get-started/evaluation-and-caching). The Worker advertises the interval via `X-Poll-Interval` and the SDK auto-adjusts, so a plan change re-paces your fleet without a redeploy.

## Resource caps

Team members and Autopilot scheduled runs are **unlimited on every tier**. Free caps capability (10 flags / configs / kill switches); Pro and above lift those caps. Experiments & universes are **Business+**. `Unlimited` means no plan-level cap (platform fair-use ceilings still apply for abuse protection).

| Resource                      | Free      | Pro       | Business  | Enterprise |
| ----------------------------- | --------- | --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Feature flags (gates)         | 10        | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Dynamic configs               | 10        | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Kill switches                 | 10        | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Team members                  | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Bugs & feature requests       | 1,000     | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Autopilot scheduled runs / mo | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Running experiments           | —         | —         | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Universes                     | —         | —         | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Metrics                       | 10        | 20        | 50        | Unlimited  |
| Event catalog entries         | 50        | 250       | 1,000     | Unlimited  |
| SDK keys                      | 5         | 50        | 200       | Unlimited  |

## Included monthly usage & overage

Event usage is **notify-only** on paid plans — events are never dropped or rejected. When a project crosses 80% / 100% of its monthly credit, the usage cron notifies you (and ops, for paid projects). Free is hard-capped per day for abuse protection. Production error events (SEE) and experiment/gate-test exposures **share the events meter** — there is no separate per-error or per-exposure price. Flag evaluations carry their own monthly credit, then per-million overage on paid tiers.

| Allowance / rate    | Free             | Pro                     | Business                | Enterprise |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------- |
| Events / month      | 100K (daily cap) | 100K, then **$1 / M**   | 1M, then **$0.75 / M**  | Committed  |
| Evaluations / month | 1.5M (daily cap) | 10M, then **$0.10 / M** | 50M, then **$0.05 / M** | Committed  |
| Included ops seats  | Unlimited        | 3, then $7/seat         | 5, then $12/seat        | Custom     |
| Jarvis credits      | Pay-as-you-go    | $3 / mo, then credits   | $3 / mo, then credits   | Custom     |

## Retention

| Data                          | Free     | Pro      | Business  | Enterprise |
| ----------------------------- | -------- | -------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Experiment results            | ~30 days | ~90 days | ~180 days | ~365 days  |
| Raw events (Analytics Engine) | ~90 days | ~90 days | ~90 days  | ~90 days   |
| Audit log                     | ~7 days  | ~30 days | ~180 days | ~365 days  |

## Feature availability

Experiments with **basic stats** (Welch t-test, SRM) and exposure logging land at **Business**. **Advanced statistics** — anything that needs pre-experiment data storage or extra computation (CUPED, mSPRT sequential testing, custom α) — plus SSO/RBAC and data warehouse export land at **Enterprise**.

<Callout type="info" title="Sequential testing & holdouts">
  **Sequential testing** lets you peek at results without inflating false positives — an
  **Enterprise** feature. **Holdouts** live on the universe (a fixed segment excluded from every
  experiment in it), a **Business** feature. SRM detection (sample-ratio-mismatch guard) is on for
  everyone.
</Callout>

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and re-pace your fleet through KV — nothing to redeploy. Manage your plan in **Project → Billing**, where you'll also see live usage against every limit on this page.
