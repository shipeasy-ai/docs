Plan levers are platform knobs we control centrally — they live in the plan config, not per project. Changing a plan value re-paces every project on that plan through the next KV rebuild, with no per-project migration.

<Callout type="warn" title="Numbers here are illustrative">
  The tables below reflect current defaults but can move. **Your dashboard shows your live limits
  and usage** — check **Project → Billing** for the authoritative numbers and where you stand
  against them.
</Callout>

## Read path

The biggest functional difference between tiers is how often the SDK refreshes its rules.

| Knob               | Free                          | Pro                           | Business                      | Enterprise                    |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Poll interval      | ~300s                         | ~60s                          | ~30s                          | ~10s                          |
| Analysis frequency | Daily                         | Daily                         | Daily                         | Daily                         |
| Propagation model  | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge |

The poll interval is the worst-case lag between an edit going live at the edge and a given SDK instance noticing it on its next background poll. Evaluation itself is always local and instant — see [Evaluation & caching](/get-started/evaluation-and-caching). The Worker advertises the interval via `X-Poll-Interval` and the SDK auto-adjusts, so a plan change re-paces your fleet without a redeploy.

## Resource caps

Flags, configs, and team members are **unlimited on every tier** — we cap usage (events), not capability. `Unlimited` means no plan-level cap (platform fair-use ceilings still apply for abuse protection).

| Resource              | Free      | Pro       | Business  | Enterprise |
| --------------------- | --------- | --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Feature flags (gates) | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Dynamic configs       | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Team members          | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Ops issues            | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Autopilot runs / mo   | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Running experiments   | 3         | 10        | 25        | Unlimited  |
| Groups per experiment | 4         | 10        | 20        | Unlimited  |
| Universes             | 3         | 10        | 25        | Unlimited  |
| Metrics               | 25        | 100       | 500       | Unlimited  |
| Event catalog entries | 50        | 250       | 1,000     | Unlimited  |
| SDK keys              | 10        | 50        | 200       | Unlimited  |
| i18n keys             | 1,000     | 10,000    | 50,000    | Unlimited  |
| i18n locale profiles  | 2         | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |

## Included monthly usage & overage

Event usage is **notify-only** on paid plans — events are never dropped or rejected. When a project crosses 80% / 100% of its monthly credit, the usage cron notifies you (and ops, for paid projects). **Flag evaluations are never metered.** Free is hard-capped per day for abuse protection.

| Allowance / rate    | Free           | Pro                  | Business                 | Enterprise    |
| ------------------- | -------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------- |
| Events / month      | 1M (daily cap) | 20M, then **$1 / M** | 100M, then **$0.75 / M** | Committed     |
| Flag evaluations    | Anti-abuse cap | Never metered        | Never metered            | Never metered |
| Included ops seats  | Unlimited      | 3, then $7/seat      | 5, then $12/seat         | Custom        |
| AI-translated words | 5K             | 50K                  | 250K                     | Custom        |

## Retention

| Data                          | Free     | Pro      | Business  | Enterprise |
| ----------------------------- | -------- | -------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Experiment results            | ~30 days | ~90 days | ~180 days | ~365 days  |
| Raw events (Analytics Engine) | ~90 days | ~90 days | ~90 days  | ~90 days   |
| Audit log                     | ~7 days  | ~30 days | ~180 days | ~365 days  |

## Feature availability

Statistics land at **Pro**; governance (SAML/SSO, RBAC, audit, holdouts) lands at **Business** — never behind an enterprise-only paywall.

| Feature                       | Free | Pro | Business | Enterprise |
| ----------------------------- | ---- | --- | -------- | ---------- |
| SRM detection                 | ✅   | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| MCP access                    | ✅   | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| CUPED variance reduction      | —    | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| Sequential testing            | —    | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| Custom significance threshold | —    | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| Change approvals              | —    | ✅  | ✅       | ✅         |
| Holdout groups                | —    | —   | ✅       | ✅         |
| Data / warehouse export       | —    | —   | ✅       | ✅         |
| SAML / SSO                    | —    | —   | ✅       | ✅         |
| Custom RBAC roles             | —    | —   | ✅       | ✅         |
| SCIM provisioning             | —    | —   | —        | ✅         |

<Callout type="info" title="Sequential testing & holdouts">
  **Sequential testing** lets you peek at results without inflating false positives — available from
  **Pro**. **Holdouts** live on the universe (a fixed segment excluded from every experiment in it),
  a **Business** feature. SRM detection (sample-ratio-mismatch guard) is on for everyone.
</Callout>

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and re-pace your fleet through KV — nothing to redeploy. Manage your plan in **Project → Billing**, where you'll also see live usage against every limit on this page.
