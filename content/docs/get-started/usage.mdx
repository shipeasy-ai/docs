The **Usage** page (`/dashboard/<projectId>/usage`) shows how much your project reads from Shipeasy: per-evaluation activity over the last **30 days**, counted at the edge from SDK telemetry beacons.

<Callout type="info" title="Counts are sampled and daily">
  Usage is approximate (sampled) and refreshes **once daily**. It's an activity view, not a billing
  meter — nothing on this page rejects or drops traffic.
</Callout>

## What's counted

Reads are bucketed into five features, each with its own headline tile and daily sparkline, plus a **Total reads** tile:

| Feature           | Counts                  |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Feature flags** | Gate evaluations.       |
| **Configs**       | Config reads.           |
| **Killswitches**  | Killswitch checks.      |
| **Experiments**   | Experiment assignments. |
| **Events**        | Logged events ingested. |

Below the tiles, **Busiest resources** lists your highest-volume individual flags/configs/experiments/events with a per-resource sparkline and total, so you can see which specific resource is driving the numbers.

## Included credit & quotas

Some plans include a monthly allowance of events and evaluations. When you cross **80%** of the included credit for the month, a banner appears on this page showing usage vs the allowance; at 100% the banner turns red.

<Callout type="warn" title="Nothing is dropped">
  Exceeding the included credit is **informational only** — evaluations and events keep flowing. The
  banner is a heads-up to consider upgrading if the volume is your new normal.
</Callout>

Other limits (number of flags, configs, running experiments, universes, i18n keys, SDK keys, team seats) are hard caps rather than usage meters — see [Plan limits](/get-started/plan-limits) for the full table and the [Billing tab](/get-started/modules#billing--plan-limits) for your current plan's numbers.
