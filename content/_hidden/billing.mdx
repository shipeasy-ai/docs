Everything billing lives in one place: **Project → Settings → Billing**. Your subscription, payment method, plan limits, live usage, and the Jarvis assistant's credit balance are all managed from that tab — there is no separate billing flow.

<Callout type="warn" title="The dashboard is authoritative">
  Prices and allowances below are illustrative and can change. The **Billing** tab shows your live
  plan, the exact amounts, and where your usage stands against every limit. Treat it as the source
  of truth.
</Callout>

## Plans

| Plan           | Price                                    | For                                                                            |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Free**       | $0 / forever                             | Side projects and solo work — 10 flags/configs/kill switches, no card.         |
| **Pro**        | $49 / mo (or $39 / mo billed annually)   | Teams shipping weekly — unlimited flags, agents auto-triggered on new issues.  |
| **Business**   | $199 / mo (or $159 / mo billed annually) | The experimentation tier — unlimited experiments & universes with basic stats. |
| **Enterprise** | Contact sales                            | Advanced stats (CUPED, mSPRT, custom α), warehouse export, SSO/RBAC/SCIM, SLA. |

Team members and **Autopilot scheduled runs are unlimited on every tier**. Analytics events and flag evaluations each carry a generous monthly credit, then metered overage at published per-million rates (see [overage](#usage--overage)). Pro and Business include a **14-day free trial**. The full picture — resource caps, retention, governance, and tier-gated features — is on the [pricing page](https://shipeasy.ai/pricing) and in [Plan limits & quotas](/get-started/plan-limits).

## Upgrading

Pick **Pro** or **Business** in the plan picker and check out — payment runs through Stripe, and the upgrade takes effect **immediately**. Moving between Pro and Business is prorated instantly. There's nothing to redeploy: changing plans re-paces your fleet through the next KV rebuild. Once subscribed, **Manage billing** opens the Stripe customer portal, where you can update your card, download invoices, or cancel.

## Monthly vs annual

Toggle the billing interval in the plan picker. Annual is cheaper per month but billed once a year. Switching **monthly → annual** or **annual → monthly** is **deferred to the end of your current period** — your existing plan stays active until then, with no refund for the remaining term. A banner on the Billing tab shows any scheduled switch and lets you cancel it before it applies.

## Usage & overage

Paid plans include large monthly **event** and **flag evaluation** allowances; past them you're billed for the overage at a COGS-anchored, per-million rate. Exposure events (from experiments and gate A/B tests) count toward the events meter and bill at the event overage rate.

| Metered usage       | Free             | Pro                     | Business                |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Events / month      | 100K (daily cap) | 100K, then **$1 / M**   | 1M, then **$0.75 / M**  |
| Evaluations / month | 1.5M (daily cap) | 10M, then **$0.10 / M** | 50M, then **$0.05 / M** |

<Callout type="info" title="Nothing is ever dropped">
  Event usage is **notify-only** on paid plans — events always flow, even past your included credit.
  You're notified at 80% and 100%, and any overage is billed at the rates above via Stripe metered
  usage. The [Usage](/get-started/usage) page shows your activity over the last 30 days.
</Callout>

## Downgrading

Downgrading to Free is **scheduled to the end of your billing period** — paid features keep working until then, and the Billing tab shows the effective date with an option to resume. Before the switch lands, the dashboard previews what a downgrade would affect (running experiments, enabled flags, and live configs over the Free caps) so there are no surprises.

## Jarvis assistant credits

The in-dashboard **Jarvis** assistant is metered separately with **prepaid credits**, not the subscription fee. Each plan ships with a monthly credit allowance; you can top up anytime from the same Billing tab (larger packs include bonus credits, and purchased credits never expire). Read-only browsing is cheap; longer reasoning, doc search, and multi-step setup flows cost more. When the balance hits zero the assistant pauses its write and reasoning actions and prompts you to top up — your flags, experiments, and data are unaffected.

See [Credits & metering](/assistant/credits) for exactly how credits are deducted and what happens at zero.
