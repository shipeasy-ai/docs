Plan levers are platform knobs we control centrally — they live in the plan config, not per project. Changing a plan value re-paces every project on that plan through the next KV rebuild, with no per-project migration.

<Callout type="warn" title="Numbers here are illustrative">
  The tables below reflect current defaults but can move. **Your dashboard shows your live limits
  and usage** — check **Project → Billing** for the authoritative numbers and where you stand
  against them.
</Callout>

## Read path

The biggest functional difference between tiers is how often the SDK refreshes its rules.

| Knob              | Free                          | Pro                           | Business                      | Enterprise                    |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Poll interval     | ~300s                         | ~60s                          | ~30s                          | ~30s                          |
| Propagation model | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge | Infinite-TTL + explicit purge |

30s is the platform floor — no tier polls faster than that.

The poll interval is the worst-case lag between an edit going live at the edge and a given SDK instance noticing it on its next background poll. Evaluation itself is always local and instant — see [Evaluation & caching](/get-started/evaluation-and-caching). The Worker advertises the interval via `X-Poll-Interval` and the SDK auto-adjusts, so a plan change re-paces your fleet without a redeploy.

## Resource caps

Team members and Autopilot runs are **unlimited on every tier** — seats are never counted or
billed. Free caps capability (10 flags / configs / kill switches); Pro and above lift those caps.
`Unlimited` means no plan-level cap (platform fair-use ceilings still apply for abuse protection).

| Resource                | Free      | Pro       | Business  | Enterprise |
| ----------------------- | --------- | --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Feature flags (gates)   | 10        | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Dynamic configs         | 10        | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Kill switches           | 10        | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Metrics                 | 10        | 50        | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Alert rules             | 5         | 25        | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Event catalog entries   | 50        | 250       | 1,000     | Unlimited  |
| SDK keys                | 5         | 50        | 200       | Unlimited  |
| Team members            | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Bugs & feature requests | 1,000     | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |
| Autopilot runs / mo     | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited  |

## Included monthly usage & overage

Event usage is **notify-only** on paid plans — events are never dropped or rejected. When a project
crosses 80% / 100% of its monthly credit, the usage cron notifies you (and ops, for paid projects).
Free is hard-capped per day for abuse protection. Production error events (SEE) **share the events
meter** — there is no separate per-error price. Gate exposures are counted with flag evaluations,
which carry their own monthly credit.

Overage rates are **identical on every paid tier**: Business is a features tier, not a volume
discount, so the cost lines stay parallel.

| Allowance / rate    | Free             | Pro                     | Business                | Enterprise |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------- |
| Price / month       | $0               | $49                     | $199                    | Custom     |
| Events / month      | 100K (daily cap) | 1M, then **$5 / M**     | 10M, then **$5 / M**    | Committed  |
| Evaluations / month | 1.5M (daily cap) | 10M, then **$0.10 / M** | 50M, then **$0.10 / M** | Committed  |
| Jarvis credits      | Pay-as-you-go    | $3 / mo, then credits   | $3 / mo, then credits   | Custom     |

## Retention

Retention is a real tier lever, enforced at read time — an older window is not queryable even
while the underlying rows are still within Cloudflare's own three-month store.

| Data                          | Free    | Pro      | Business  | Enterprise |
| ----------------------------- | ------- | -------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Raw events (Analytics Engine) | ~7 days | ~30 days | ~90 days  | ~90 days   |
| Audit log                     | ~7 days | ~30 days | ~180 days | ~365 days  |
